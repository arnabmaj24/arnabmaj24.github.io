The data came from EuRoC MAV ROS bags: 20 Hz camera frames matched to 100 Hz VICON poses (within 1 ms), cut into about 12,000 sequences, split 60/20/20, with noise and brightness augmentation. TSFormer-VO uses joint space-time attention (12 blocks, 8 heads); DeepVO is a CNN feeding a 2-layer LSTM. The honest result is that neither tracked well and both drifted badly. DeepVO stayed closer to the true trajectory in position, while TSFormer was better at predicting roll.
