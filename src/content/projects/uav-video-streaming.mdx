At ~4 FPS, the ground-station video feed was closer to a slow slideshow than a live view, not good enough to actually fly or make decisions off of in real time. I profiled the pipeline end to end (onboard capture, the ROS nodes moving frames around, and the link out over 4G) to find where frames were actually being lost or delayed, rather than guessing at a single bottleneck, and got it to roughly 24 FPS. The hard part wasn't throughput on a bench, it was staying usable on an actual 4G connection in the field, where bandwidth and latency aren't constant the way they are in a lab.
