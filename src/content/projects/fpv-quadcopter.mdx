Three of us are building a battery-powered FPV quadcopter with one goal: beat the world speed record. The body is shaped with ANSYS aerodynamic analysis, and the airframe is made of 3D-printed parts optimized for weight. It has reached 104 km/h so far, and the project is ongoing.
