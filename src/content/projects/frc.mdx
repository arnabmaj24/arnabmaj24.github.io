## What FRC is

FIRST Robotics Competition teams get a few weeks to design and build a robot for that season's game, then compete against other teams in alliances. It's a small engineering company run by high-school students: mechanical, electrical, software, and a deadline that doesn't move.

## The robot

I owned the electrical side: I designed the robot's electrical system and control circuits and verified them before competition. I also chose the motors and gear ratios for the drivetrain and mechanisms, using planetary, belt, chain and spur, and harmonic reductions depending on the job.

On the control side, I tuned position, velocity and acceleration-based PID loops on the motor controllers. The acceleration-based loops kept error from building up.

## Results

As captain, I led the team to its first competition win and finals appearance in 8 years, and we moved up 25 places in the provincial ranking from 2022 to 2023.

## Mentoring

After I captained, I stayed on as a mentor until June 2025, teaching new members CAD and electrical design.
